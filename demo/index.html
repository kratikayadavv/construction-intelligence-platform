HIGH RISKSafety score · 58/100

Potential PPE and edge-protection issues need on-site verification before work proceeds.

  • PPE: Confirm all workers use suitable helmets and high-visibility clothing.
  • Fall protection: Inspect scaffold platforms, guardrails, access and ties.
  • Housekeeping: Clear materials from walkways and access points.

Immediate action: Restrict access to any unprotected edge until the competent site lead has inspected it.

🔍 Crack Detection

Sample visual triage for a diagonal crack near a window opening.
HIGH · EXAMPLEVisual width: not measured

Sample diagnostic summary

A diagonal crack near an opening can have several causes, including movement or stress concentration. A photo alone cannot confirm its cause or structural significance.

  • Document crack location and date, and monitor changes.
  • Look for related signs such as displacement, water ingress or continuing movement.
  • Arrange an on-site assessment by a licensed structural engineer before choosing a repair.

Urgency: Prompt professional review is recommended in this illustrative scenario.
